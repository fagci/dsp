### Baud Estimator

`blindBaud` · used in 1 preset

The symbol rate of any IQ or real stream. Symbol edges give the spectrum of four features — the envelope, jumps of the envelope, jumps of the frequency, jumps of the complex sample — a line at the symbol rate (random NRZ data has no such line itself, the jumps do).

- **In:** `in` (iq)
- **Out:** `baud` (num), `conf` (num), `sps` (num), `text` (txt)
- **Parameters:** `min` — lowest symbol rate to look for, Bd; `max` — highest symbol rate to look for, Bd; `avg` — spectrum averaging, frames; `thr` — peak above the noise to accept, dB

### PSK/QAM Receiver (EVM)

`pskRx` · used in 1 preset

An IQ stream of a BPSK / QPSK / 8PSK / 16QAM carrier (shifted near zero, ≥ 2 samples per symbol) → root-raised-cosine matched filter → symbol AGC → Gardner timing recovery with cubic interpolation → carrier recovery: a coarse offset from the M-th power of the symbols (for 16QAM only the corner points, whose 4th power is constant) while unlocked, then a second-order decision-directed loop.

- **In:** `in` (iq)
- **Out:** `out` (iq), `evm` (num), `mer` (num), `foff` (num), `lock` (num)
- **Parameters:** `mod` — modulation; `baud` — symbol rate, Bd (≥ 2 samples per symbol at the input); `alpha` — RRC roll-off; `tbw` — timing loop bandwidth, × symbol rate; `cbw` — carrier loop bandwidth, × symbol rate; `win` — EVM window, symbols

### SINAD / THD+N

`sinad` · used in 1 preset

The standard receiver test. The tone is taken out by a least-squares sine fit (cos, sin and a constant) over the whole window, the frequency is found by a golden-section search around the FFT peak, so even 80 dB gets no leakage into the residual; what is left in the band is noise and distortion.

- **In:** `in` (sig), `freq` (num)
- **Out:** `sinad` (num), `thdn` (num), `tone` (num), `level` (num), `resid` (num)
- **Parameters:** `freq` — tone, Hz (0 = find the strongest in the band); `band` — measurement band; `win` — window, s

### Spectrum Baseline

`specBase` · used in 1 preset

Remembers what the band normally looks like and reports the difference — a watchman for hours of unattended work. The air is cut into cells (*cell width*, `auto` = the bin width on the 1-2-5 grid; a sweep or a retuned source just adds cells, the norm is kept).

- **In:** `spec` (spec)
- **Out:** `bands` (bands), `rec` (rec), `base` (spec), `count` (num), `f` (num), `excess` (num), `learned` (num)
- **Parameters:** `mode` — monitor: report deviations; learn only: just update the norm; `res` — cell width, Hz (auto — the bin width of the spectrum on the 1-2-5 grid); `learn` — visits to learn a cell before it is checked; `adapt` — norm adapts over, visits (a permanent new station is absorbed after ≈ 2× this); `thr` — report above the norm by, dB; `z` — … and by more than this many σ of the cell; `sdMin` — smallest σ of a cell, dB (a quiet cell is not over-sensitive); `confirm` — frames in a row before a report; `hold` — event ends after it is gone for, ms; `wide` — wider than this, kHz — a wideband event (jammer, raised floor); `missing` — report carriers that were almost always present and vanished

### Nipkow Disk: Receive

`nipkowRx` · used in 4 presets

- **In:** `level` (sig), `sync` (sig), `trim` (num), `shift` (num)
- **Out:** `img` (img), `lock` (num), `rpm` (num)
- **Parameters:** `std` — disk: a ready-made variant (fills in the fields below; then edit freely); `lines` — lines per field = holes on one spiral; `spirals` — spirals = fields per turn of the disk; `interlace` — interlace: spirals are shifted by 1/k of the line pitch, a frame is k fields (otherwise every field is a whole picture); `color` — colour: red, green and blue pictures go in turn (a filter wheel), one colour frame = 3 pictures (3 frames when interlaced); the start of the cycle is marked by the sync; `fps` — pictures per second, each colour plane counts (turns per second = fps ÷ spirals; ×60 = rpm for one spiral); `px` — pixels per line (auto: about 4 samples per pixel; both ends must agree); `aspect` — window width : height — sets the inner radius of the spiral; `sweep` — the first line of a field is at the outer or inner edge of the spiral; `rot` — direction of rotation (mirrors the picture in the disk geometry); `sync` — sync: pulse — separate signal once per turn; in-band — an extra hole before every field, below black; none/free/auto — no sync; `view` — picture: flat — straight lines; disk — as seen through the disk: lines are arcs of the spiral (a sector); `size` — output picture width, px (height follows the window shape); `invert` — negative; `auto` — auto level: stretch the picture to the full range; `trim` — free / auto start: turn period trim, ppm (the picture stops sliding when it matches the transmitter); `shift` — picture shift along the field, fraction (needed in free and auto: no sync marks where the field begins)

### Object Detect

`objdet` · used in 1 preset

Finds objects (80 COCO classes: person, car, dog, cat, phone…) in a photo or video. Inputs: `vid` (live video — Camera, Video) and `img` (photo, IP Camera frames); a photo is detected once (again when a setting changes), video every *detect every* seconds.

- **In:** `vid` (vid), `img` (img)
- **Out:** `img` (img), `text` (txt), `go` (num), `rec` (rec), `count` (num), `total` (num)
- **Parameters:** `score` — min confidence; `classes` — only these classes, e.g. person, car (empty — all 80); `period` — video: pause between detections, s (more — lighter on CPU); `track` — tracking: id per object; rec/go only for new ones; `age` — tracking: keep a lost object, s; `boxes` — draw boxes on the output picture; `dsize` — detection size, px (smaller — lighter on CPU); `maxw` — picture width, px
